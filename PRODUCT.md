# Margevia
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User accepted the recommended lightweight website with an interactive sample demo, changing the language to English. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript; public Vercel hosting with source on GitHub. Journal HTML is generated locally from checked-in content, then committed; Vercel still publishes dist/ without a build step.

## Users
Purchasing and operations managers at small catering and restaurant groups.

## Product Purpose
Proposed supplier invoice reconciliation product: compare agreed prices, deliveries and invoices; explain discrepancies; track credits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This deliverable includes a landing page, interactive browser-local demonstration using explicitly fictional sample records, and a public journal with four practical guides and two product-building stories. The journal index and six articles have permanent anonymous-access static routes, subject-specific editorial illustrations and unique share images. Editorial examples do not establish actual recovered money, customer evidence or a founder biography. No live OCR, authentication, accounting integrations, customer inbox or actual invoice upload is implemented or claimed. Pilot planning collects contact details, team, review focus and location choices. Direct brief delivery uses a Vercel handler and a Make workflow connected to support@margevia.solutions in Adham’s workspace. Sending is enabled only when the delivery endpoint is configured; success requires Gmail acceptance. No brief is downloaded. Prices, testimonials, customer logos, security certifications and ROI benchmarks are not established and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments
Margevia. English. User explicitly selected Nanonets as the design reference and requested its visual design adapted with Margevia copywriting. Code-first approach confirmed.

## Product Status
Customer access and market differentiation remain unvalidated. No real customers or measurable product results are established.

## Product Principles
Show the source of a discrepancy. Keep human review explicit. Separate flagged amounts from recovered money. Make the sample understandable before asking for commitment.
