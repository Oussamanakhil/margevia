Purchasing and operations managers at small catering and restaurant groups.

## Product Purpose
Proposed supplier invoice reconciliation product: compare agreed prices, deliveries and invoices; explain discrepancies; track credits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This deliverable includes a landing page, interactive browser-local demonstration using explicitly fictional sample records, a public journal with four practical guides and two product-building stories, and a public founder profile at /founder/. The journal index and six articles have permanent anonymous-access static routes, subject-specific editorial illustrations and unique share images. Editorial examples do not establish actual recovered money or customer evidence. The founder profile uses the biography and LinkedIn URL confirmed by the user on 23 November 2026; it identifies Adham Ait reqba as Margevia’s founder and full-stack developer, with ongoing EPSI studies and a STATION F Entrepreneur in Residence role beginning November 2026. No live OCR, authentication, accounting integrations, customer inbox or actual invoice upload is implemented or claimed. Pilot planning collects contact details, team, review focus and location choices. Direct brief delivery uses a Vercel handler and a Make workflow connected to support@margevia.solutions in Adham’s workspace. Sending is enabled only when the delivery endpoint is configured; success requires Gmail acceptance. No brief is downloaded. Prices, testimonials, customer logos, security certifications and ROI benchmarks are not established and must not be fabricated.

The user confirmed three pilot users on 23 November 2026. Their participation does not establish paying customers, revenue, recovered money or measurable product outcomes. ## Product Principles
