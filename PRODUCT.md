# Margevia
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User accepted the recommended lightweight website with an interactive sample demo, changing the language to English. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript; public Vercel hosting with source on GitHub. Journal HTML is generated locally from checked-in content, then committed; Vercel still publishes dist/ without a build step.

## Users
Purchasing and operations managers at small catering and restaurant groups.

## Product Purpose
Proposed supplier invoice reconciliation product: compare agreed prices, deliveries and invoices; explain discrepancies; track credits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This deliverable includes a landing page, interactive browser-local demonstration using explicitly fictional sample records, and a public journal with four practical guides and two product-building stories. The journal index and six articles have permanent anonymous-access static routes, subject-specific editorial illustrations and unique share images. Editorial examples do not establish actual recovered money, customer evidence or a founder biography. No live OCR, authentication, accounting integrations, customer inbox, outbound messages or actual invoice upload is implemented or claimed. Pilot planning allows local team, review-focus and location choices. Send brief is disabled pending a recipient and delivery setup; no brief is downloaded and no information is sent. Prices, testimonials, customer logos, security certifications and ROI benchmarks are not established and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments
Margevia. English. User explicitly selected Nanonets as the design reference and requested its visual design adapted with Margevia copywriting. Code-first approach confirmed.

## Product Status
Customer access and market differentiation remain unvalidated. No real customers or measurable product results are established.

## Product Principles
Show the source of a discrepancy. Keep human review explicit. Separate flagged amounts from recovered money. Make the sample understandable before asking for commitment.
