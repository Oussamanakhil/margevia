# Margevia
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User accepted the recommended lightweight website with an interactive sample demo, changing the language to English. Buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript; Public Vercel hosting with source on GitHub.

## Users
Purchasing and operations managers at small catering and restaurant groups.

## Product Purpose
Proposed supplier invoice reconciliation product: compare agreed prices, deliveries and invoices; explain discrepancies; track credits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This deliverable is a landing page and interactive browser-local demonstration using explicitly fictional sample records. No live OCR, authentication, accounting integrations, customer inbox, outbound messages or actual invoice upload is implemented or claimed. Pilot planning allows local team, review-focus and location choices. Send brief is disabled pending a recipient and delivery setup; no brief is downloaded and no information is sent. Prices, testimonials, customer logos, security certifications and ROI benchmarks are not established and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments
Margevia. English. User explicitly selected Nanonets as the design reference and requested its visual design adapted with Margevia copywriting. Code-first approach confirmed.

## Product Status
Customer access and market differentiation remain unvalidated. No real customers or measurable product results are established.

## Product Principles
Show the source of a discrepancy. Keep human review explicit. Separate flagged amounts from recovered money. Make the sample understandable before asking for commitment.
