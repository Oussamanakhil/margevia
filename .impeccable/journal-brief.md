# Margevia journal
Mode: Read; homepage introduction extends the existing Persuade surface.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A practical purchasing desk, combining usable invoice-review guidance with honest notes on the product being built.
OWN-WORLD: Preserve the user-pinned Nanonets-inspired Margevia system: DM Sans, warm paper, navy, violet, white reading surfaces and hairline frames. Commission six original physical-paper editorial illustrations, each tied to its subject.
STORY: Discover a relevant article, understand a concrete review practice, then try the fictional dashboard. Four guides and two building stories; no invented biography, customer claims or actual recovery results.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large regular-weight journal title opposite a compact introduction; below, a broad featured story with paper-bridge artwork on the left and headline on the right. Article pages put title, deck, byline and a panoramic cover before a narrow reading column and restrained contents rail. Mobile stacks the composition.
FORM: Code-first extension of the pinned visual reference. Signature interaction is quiet image enlargement on card hover and native article wayfinding; no reading-progress decoration or repetitive entrance animation. Six independent permanent article routes and unique social previews.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Verification scope: desktop 1440 and phone 390 screenshots for journal index, representative article, and homepage section; narrow 320 and tablet 768 overflow checks; permanent links, copy link, mobile navigation, local metadata and all public social images. Homepage, index and article sharing metadata is static HTML.

## Implemented evidence
The completed surface keeps Read mode: generous prose measure, flat editorial cards, a sticky desktop contents rail that becomes native in-flow wayfinding on small screens, and a quiet card-image hover. Four practical guides and two building stories are generated from content/articles.json through scripts/build-journal.py into /blog/ and six permanent article routes. The homepage introduction remains Persuade mode.

Six independent AI-generated artworks have subject-specific alt text and responsive 600px/1200px WebP exports. Prompt/source provenance is recorded in content/image-sources.json and embedded in the raster exports. Optimized sources and index/article social SVG masters are checked in. Eight unique 1200x630 sharing JPEGs cover home, index and all six articles. Static canonical/Open Graph/Twitter metadata, article BlogPosting data and sitemap entries support public anonymous-access sharing. The live WhatsApp app has not been tested.

The review finding about mobile comparison-table discoverability is resolved in the implementation: a visible swipe hint is linked to each focusable table region with aria-describedby. Final review disposition is recorded by the reviewer separately. No fabricated biography, customer outcomes or actual recovery claims are introduced.
